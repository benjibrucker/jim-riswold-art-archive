# Jim Riswold (1957–2024) — Complete Art Inventory & Estate Catalog

An interactive, encyclopedic digital catalogue raisonné and physical inventory index for contemporary artist **Jim Riswold**.

Curated in collaboration with **Jake Riswold**, **Haley Riswold**, **Peter Yue**, and **Guy Overfelt** (*Four Oceans, One Family* gallery).

## Overview

Jim Riswold was a legendary American creative director (Wieden+Kennedy: Michael Jordan & Spike Lee / Mars Blackmon, "Bo Knows", Bugs Bunny) who, after surviving leukemia in 2000, dedicated his life to satirical fine art. His photographic and sculptural works deflated monumental dictators, tyrants, and art icons through the subversion of miniature toy figurines.

This digital index reconciles the complete physical estate collection held across storage vaults, rooms, and flat files.

## Key Features

- **Wikipedia Design Reference:** Neutral, readable Vector-style layout with detailed Infobox metadata cards.
- **Autofill Search:** Dynamic autocomplete search indexing every title, series name, subject, and accession code.
- **Fast Status Pills:** Immediate one-click isolation of Available, Pending/Review, and Sold pieces.
- **Genre & Series Filters:** Dictators & Tyrants, War & Military, Art History & Parody, Philosophy & Religion, Medicine & Mortality, American Politics, and Sculptures.
- **Dual Display Modes:** Gallery Grid (visual card view) and Archive Table (dense spreadsheet view with sortable columns).
- **Detail Inspection Modal:** High-resolution zoomable viewer, full provenance, storage dimensions vs. paper dimensions, signature, framing status, and direct email inquiry to Jake & Haley.
- **Export Tools:** Instant client-side export to CSV and JSON.
