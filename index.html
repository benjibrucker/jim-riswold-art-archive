James Paul Riswold (December 7, 1957 – August 9, 2024) was an acclaimed American creative director for advertising agency Wieden+Kennedy and later a prolific contemporary satirical artist. In his 14 years at W+K, he conceived legendary campaigns including the pairing of Michael Jordan and Spike Lee (Mars Blackmon), Bugs Bunny & Hare Jordan, Bo Jackson’s "Bo Knows", and Tiger Woods’ "I Am Tiger Woods". In 2013, he was inducted into The One Club’s Creative Hall of Fame.

Following a diagnosis of leukemia in 2000, Riswold retired from advertising to dedicate his life to fine art, humorously remarking that he moved from "a career of selling people things they don't need to making things that people don't want."

His photographic and sculptural work poked fierce, absurd fun at history’s most taboo dictators, tyrants, and monumental figures—including Adolf Hitler, Hermann Göring, Joseph Stalin, Mao Zedong, and Vladimir Putin—as well as modern pop culture and art history icons (Jeff Koons, Damien Hirst, Roy Lichtenstein). By staging small toy figurines in solemn, museum-scale environments, Riswold deflated megalomania: "Instead of providing grand expositions mythologizing the dictator, toys, by definition, make their subjects seem small, childish, and trifling."

Following his passing on August 9, 2024, his estate is actively managed by his children, Jake Riswold and Haley Riswold, in collaboration with long-time gallery partners Peter Yue and Guy Overfelt.

Exhibition History & Four Oceans, One Family Gallery

Prior to his passing, Jim Riswold presented his final lifetime art exhibition through Four Oceans, One Family, a collaborative gallery endeavor founded by Peter Yue, Guy Overfelt, and Benji Brucker.

This digital archive provides the comprehensive inventory master index connecting the physical artwork stored in the gallery vault, drawers, and rooms with the estate records maintained by Jake and Haley Riswold.
